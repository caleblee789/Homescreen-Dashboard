Your study day, at a glance. Home Screen Dashboard adds a study calendar with an integrated heatmap, daily progress, and useful statistics such as your Anki Streak to Anki's home screen. Current Features
  • Switch between Month and Year to explore your study history.
  • See today's progress, remaining cards, study time, and estimated finish time.
  • Track your last seven days, retention, and study streaks.
  • Hover over future dates to check due cards, with optional calendar indicators.
  • Add your own events and see what's coming up.
  • Choose themes, calendar colors, text size, and study preferences.
  • Keep a Bible verse beside your selected date and event details

  • Streak, navigation, event editing, and loading states use the final dashboard styling.
  • All dashboard sections remain visible; old visibility choices are restored automatically.
Getting started For Anki Desktop 26.8 and newer. Install using the add-on code below, then restart Anki. Open the calendar's gear icon or Caleb M. Add-ons Settings → Home Screen Dashboard settings, make your choices, and click Save changes. After installing from AnkiWeb, get future updates through Tools → Add-ons → Check for Updates, then restart. If the dashboard lists conflicting add-ons, disable those entries and restart. Your cards and review history are not changed. This add-on runs in Anki Desktop; it does not add a dashboard to AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid, or the AnkiWeb study page.
