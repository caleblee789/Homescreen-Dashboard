Your study day, at a glance. Home Screen Dashboard adds a study calendar, daily progress, and useful statistics to Anki's home screen. What you can do
  • Switch between Month and Year to explore your study history.
  • See today's progress, remaining cards, study time, and estimated finish time.
  • Track your last seven days, retention, and study streaks.
  • Hover over future dates to check due cards, with optional calendar indicators.
  • Add your own events and see what's coming up.
  • Choose themes, calendar colors, text size, and the sections you want to see.
  • Show an optional Bible verse, with your own library and appearance settings.
See it in action Sapphire Glass in Month view, with future due indicators turned on. Customize the look in Appearance settings. What's new in 1.8.7
  • Six clear Settings pages make options easier to find.
  • Improved light and dark Settings and smaller-window layouts.
  • Progress percentages inside the bar and study time for the last seven days.
  • More accurate buried-card counts when Anki hides related cards for today's study.
  • Optional future due indicators. New installations start with them off; hover counts remain available.
  • Separate Bible Library and Display & rotation views, with a preview before saving.
Getting started For Anki Desktop 26.8. Install using the add-on code below, then restart Anki. Open the calendar's gear icon or Caleb M. Add-ons Settings → Home Screen Dashboard settings, make your choices, and click Save changes. Already installed? Choose Tools → Add-ons → Check for Updates, then restart. If the dashboard lists conflicting add-ons, disable those entries and restart. Your cards and review history are not changed. This add-on runs in Anki Desktop; it does not add a dashboard to AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid, or the AnkiWeb study page.
