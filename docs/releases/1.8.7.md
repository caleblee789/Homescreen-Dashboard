# Home Screen Dashboard 1.8.7

A study calendar, daily progress tracker, and useful statistics on Anki's
home screen. This version is for Anki Desktop 26.8.

## What's new

- Find your options more easily with six Settings pages: Dashboard,
  Appearance, Calendar, Events, Bible verse, and About & support.
- Enjoy clearer light and dark Settings, improved spacing, and better layouts
  in smaller windows.
- See your progress percentage inside the bar and your study time for the
  last seven days.
- Check upcoming due cards by hovering over a date. Calendar indicators are
  optional, and new installations start with them off.
- Get a more accurate buried-card count when Anki hides related cards for
  today's study session.
- Manage your optional Bible verse library separately from its appearance
  and rotation settings, with a preview before saving.

## Install or update

Download `home-dashboard-overhaul-1.8.7.ankiaddon` from this release. In Anki,
choose **Tools → Add-ons → Install from file**, select the download, and
restart Anki. Existing settings are kept.

If the dashboard lists conflicting add-ons, disable those entries and restart.
The dashboard does not change your cards or review history.

Open the calendar's gear icon to customize your dashboard, then click
**Save changes** when you're ready.
